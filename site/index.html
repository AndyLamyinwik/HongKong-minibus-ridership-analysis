02 / THE CENTRAL FINDING

A closer look at
a changing industry.

A shared industry.
An uneven recovery.

The combined minibus total tells only part of the story.

Between 2015 and 2025, total minibus journeys fell by 19.51%. Green Minibus journeys fell by 11.17%, while estimated Red Minibus journeys fell by 58.00%.

The recovery paths also diverged. Green Minibus journeys increased in each annual transition from 2022 to 2025; Red Minibus journeys decreased in each. By 2025, estimated Red Minibus demand stood at 49.18% of its 2019 level. The recent recovery in the combined industry was driven by Green Minibuses.

These patterns describe published passenger journeys. They do not establish why demand changed, show that the same passengers switched services, or predict either industry's future.

06 / METHODOLOGY & SOURCES

The research covers January 2015 through December 2025: 132 months per passenger series. Official Transport Department tables supply monthly passenger journeys, average daily journeys and licensed minibus stocks.

01 Scope, official sources and minibus definitions +

Green Minibuses (GMB) and Red Minibuses (RMB) are the two passenger series within Public Light Buses (PLB). PLB is their sum. Passenger journeys count aggregate trips, not unique people.

The Hong Kong Transport Department's Monthly Traffic and Transport Digest supplies Table 2.1 monthly journeys, Table 2.1S average daily journeys and Table 2.2 month-end licensed stocks. The analysis preserves 132 consecutive months from January 2015 to December 2025.

02 Annual totals, indices and the recovery benchmark +

Annual passenger flows sum twelve monthly totals in thousands. Displaying millions divides those totals by 1,000. Annual shares are ratios of annual flows, not averages of monthly percentages.

Trend indexAnnual measure ÷ its own 2015 measure × 100

Recovery2025 annual journeys ÷ own 2019 annual journeys × 100

A value of 100 is the stated benchmark. The index and recovery views deliberately use different years. Percentage-point changes compare shares directly; they are not relative percentage changes. Lines connect the annual observations; no synthetic observations or smoothing are introduced.

Complete calculation rules ↗
03 Comparison-group denominator and transport boundaries +

The comparison-group denominator is GMB + RMB + MTR railway services + franchised buses. These four components appear once each. PLB is the GMB/RMB subtotal, not an additional component. This group does not represent all Hong Kong transport; every denominator contains estimated RMB journeys.

MTR railway services combine MTR Lines, Airport Express and Light Rail. MTR Lines include cross-boundary services; Hong Kong Tramways and MTR buses are excluded. Changing service coverage makes this a published-series comparison, not a constant-network comparison.

Each contemporaneous franchised-bus component is counted once. Through June 2023, monthly totals include Citybus franchises 1 and 2 plus NWFB. From July 2023, Citybus franchise 3 replaces franchise 1 and NWFB, while franchise 2 continues. KMB, LWB and NLB remain included. Residents' services and MTR buses are excluded. Preparation preserves the total boundary across the merger.

04 Fleet stock and the licensed vehicle-day proxy +

The fleet comparison uses December month-end licensed vehicles as an annual stock snapshot. The annual proxy uses all twelve month-end stocks, weighted by each month's actual calendar days, including leap days.

Annual proxyAnnual journeys in thousands × 1,000
÷ Σ(month-end licensed vehicles × month days)

Month-end licensing stocks stand in for daily availability. They are not the operating fleet, and the proxy does not measure actual occupancy, deployment, vehicle utilization or operational productivity. RMB journey estimation uses fleet information, limiting independent interpretation of the passenger/fleet association.

05 Validation, reconciliation and publication revisions +

Preparation checks complete month/mode keys, unique observations, valid units, numeric values and estimation labels. Reconciliation checks PLB = GMB + RMB, calendar-adjusted daily/monthly consistency and comparison-group shares summing to 100%. Independent SQL and Pandas calculations agree on the core analytical outputs. Source hashes preserve traceability.

The preserved snapshot was retrieved on 11 October 2026; the dataset reports a revision date of 21 September 2026. Six older provisional annual minibus references differ from the current monthly sums. Publication updates are the best-supported explanation, but their specific reasons remain unresolved. The analysis retains one consistent current vintage.

Source reconciliation checks internal consistency, not independent passenger-count accuracy. Cross-vintage differences are not uncertainty intervals for RMB estimates. An unmarked status flag does not guarantee finality or measurement certainty.

06 Estimates, interpretation and what journeys can show +

RMB patronage is estimated using survey, fleet and other information rather than regular operator returns. The source provides no quantified confidence interval or complete parameter history. PLB totals and comparison-group denominators inherit the estimated RMB component.

Reported GMB, MTR and bus values describe published provenance, not independently audited certainty. These historical comparisons do not isolate causal effects or identify passengers switching modes. Fleet associations and period timing remain descriptive.

The full report also examines calendar-day-adjusted profiles. Month length and within-year trends must remain distinct from passenger demand; those profiles do not establish a stable seasonal mechanism.
